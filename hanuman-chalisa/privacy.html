Privacy Policy

Hanuman Chalisa — Ad Free, Offline
Developer: Veeral Kamalia · App ID: com.veeralkamalia.hanumanchalisa
Effective date: 2 October 2026

In short

This app does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app saves a few settings only on your device:

  • your recitation count and target (for example 3 of 11)
  • reading preferences — text size, theme, whether meanings are shown
  • your reminder choice and time

This information never leaves your phone and is not sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes it.

Reminders

If you turn on Tuesday & Saturday reminders, they are scheduled and shown by your phone itself. No server is involved. On Android 13 and newer the app asks for permission to show notifications; you can turn this off at any time in Android Settings.

Internet

The app works fully offline. It does not send or receive any data over the internet.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-media software, and shares no data with third parties. Google Play, which delivers the app, has its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example if a future version adds a feature that needs data — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
